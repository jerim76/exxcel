ABOUT EXCEL

Tuition designed around the learner.

Excel Tuition Centre provides private online tuition for learners who benefit from focused, individual support.

Each lesson takes place online between a tutor and one learner, allowing teaching to be adapted to the learner's current level, goals, school programme and areas that need more attention.

We support learners following a range of international curricula and examination systems.

FAQs

Questions parents often ask.

Are the lessons one-to-one?

Yes. Excel Tuition Centre focuses on private one-to-one online tuition rather than group classes.

Do you support international curricula?

Yes. We can support learners following curricula and examination systems such as Cambridge International, Pearson Edexcel, International Baccalaureate, OxfordAQA, AQA, OCR and WJEC/Eduqas.

Do you support IB students?

Yes. Support can be arranged for learners following IB programmes including PYP, MYP, Diploma Programme and Career-related Programme, depending on the subject and tutor availability.

Do you offer SAT and IELTS preparation?

Yes. We provide academic and preparation support for international tests including SAT and IELTS, as well as selected other examinations.

Can you support homeschooling?

Yes. One-to-one online tuition can supplement a homeschooling programme or provide focused support in particular subjects.

How do I get started?

Contact us by WhatsApp or email with the learner's age, subject, curriculum and the type of support you are looking for.
